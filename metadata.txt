about=Inspects and imports LandXML TIN surfaces, existing-ground profiles, corridor cross-sections, alignments, profiles, cross-sections, station points, boundaries, feature lines and breaklines. Profiles can be plotted with vertical exaggeration and labeled VPC/VPI/VPT controls, tangent grades and K values; optional schematic map profile layers appear beside the alignment using an explicitly selected CRS. OpenRoads Designer terrain structures were examined in private exports and reproduced in a synthetic regression fixture; one private OpenRoads alignment/profile export was exercised in QGIS 3.44 without redistribution or survey-control validation. Includes a Complete Road Design GeoPackage export. A Profile Viewer dock opens the existing-ground vs design long-section of an alignment clicked in the map, with shaded cut/fill, transitions and PNG/SVG/PDF/CSV/HTML export. Earthworks tools report centreline cut/fill segments and transitions, Civil 3D corridor section cut/fill volumes, material quantities, mass haul and side-slope areas for grassing, and TIN-to-TIN cut/fill rasters and volumes.

    Coordinate interpretation and transformation (stored coordinates, axis swap, offset, 2-D Helmert, or reprojection) are explicit and controlled by the user. LandXML-declared CRS is shown for reference only. US survey feet, international feet and meters remain distinct.
